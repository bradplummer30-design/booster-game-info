Privacy Policy — Padfall (iOS)

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Padfall is a single-player game for iPhone made by BRADLY MICHEAL PLUMMER-RIX ("we"). Contact: skyflip2026@gmail.com.

This policy describes Padfall. Version 1.0 contains no advertising, analytics, accounts or purchases. Starting with version 1.1, Padfall can show advertising if you allow it in the app (see section 3). The policy covers three kinds of information: your game progress and settings, which stay on your device; advertising data, which the advertising service processes only after you allow ads; and email you send us, which we hold. Each is described below with how it can be deleted.

1. What the app stores on your device

Padfall saves your game progress and settings on your device only: which campaign missions are unlocked, your best score and grade for each mission, your best Cadence total, and whether sound and screen shake are on. This information is stored in the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

2. What the app does not do

Padfall does not require an account, has no purchases and no analytics of its own. It does not ask to track you across other apps and websites, does not access your location services, camera, microphone, photos or contacts, and does not send your gameplay or settings over the internet. Version 1.0 shows no ads at all; from version 1.1, ads appear only if you choose "Allow ads" (section 3).

If a future version adds any other service that collects information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

3. Advertising (version 1.1 and later, only if you allow it)

When you first open version 1.1, Padfall asks whether you allow ads. If you choose No ads, the advertising service is never started and nothing in this section applies; the game is fully playable. If you choose Allow ads, Padfall shows a small banner on the main menu and an occasional fullscreen ad after a landing or crash (never during a flight), served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource mediation and Unity Ads, operated by Unity Technologies). You can change your choice at any time from Privacy options on the main menu; choosing No ads hides the banner and stops fullscreen ads from then on (the service that was already running stops with the next launch).

To serve and measure ads, Unity's software in the app processes information such as your device's advertising and vendor identifiers (Padfall does not request tracking permission, so the advertising identifier is not available to it), device and app information, coarse location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with ads (views, clicks). Unity uses this information under its own privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy (game-player section: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy), which also explains your choices, including Unity's data access and opt-out options. We do not receive personal information from the advertising service; we receive aggregated reporting. Padfall signals "do not sell or share" to the advertising service for everyone and does not flag the app as directed at children (it is not).

4. Email you send us, and this website

The app's Privacy and Support links open this website in your browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages themselves set no cookies and contain no analytics or third-party content. GitHub, the host, logs and stores the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub (GitHub's documentation: "When a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor has signed into GitHub or not" — docs.github.com). That logging is done by GitHub under GitHub's Privacy Statement, not by us; we do not receive those logs.

If you email us at skyflip2026@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to handle your request and reply to you. We keep correspondence about this game for at most 12 months after your request is resolved, and we delete it earlier when it is no longer needed. Two narrow exceptions: if an issue you raised is still open, we keep that thread until it is resolved, and the 12-month limit then runs from that point; and if a law requires us to keep a particular message for longer, we keep that message only for as long as the law requires.

You can ask us to delete your correspondence with us about this game at any time by emailing the same address — please put "Deletion request" in the subject line so we can find it. We handle deletion requests separately from routine support, and where a law sets a deadline for answering such a request, that deadline applies.

Deleting your data — by kind

  1. Deleting Padfall from your device removes its saved progress and settings from that device; "offloading" the app (an iOS feature) removes the app but keeps its documents and data, so progress returns if you reinstall. Backup behaviour is controlled by iOS; we make no promises about device backups.
  2. Emails you exchanged with us about this game are deleted on request at skyflip2026@gmail.com, and otherwise at most 12 months after your request is resolved — earlier when no longer needed, subject to the two narrow exceptions in section 4.

Children

Padfall is not offered in the App Store's Kids category and is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Padfall has no accounts, purchases or chat; ads (version 1.1 and later) are shown only to people who allow them and are not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
