Privacy Policy — Padfall (iOS)

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Padfall is a single-player game for iPhone and iPad made by BRADLY MICHEAL PLUMMER-RIX ("we"). Contact: skyflip2026@gmail.com.

This policy describes Padfall. Version 1.0 contains no advertising, analytics, accounts or purchases. Starting with version 1.1, Padfall is supported by advertising (see section 3) and offers optional paint schemes as in-app purchases (see section 3a). Starting with version 1.3, Padfall adds a daily challenge and flight cards that stay on your device (section 3b), optional Game Center leaderboards run by Apple (section 3c), a share button for flight cards (section 3d) and Apple's rating prompt (section 3e). The policy covers four kinds of information: your game progress, settings and flight records, which stay on your device; advertising data, which the advertising service processes to serve ads once you have answered the personalization question; leaderboard scores, which Game Center receives only if you choose to use it; and email you send us, which we hold. Each is described below with how it can be deleted.

1. What the app stores on your device

Padfall saves your game progress and settings on your device only: which campaign missions are unlocked, your best score and grade for each mission, your best Cadence total, and whether sound and screen shake are on. From version 1.3 it also keeps, on the device only: the flight card of your best landing per campaign mission (the landing's score, grade, softness, centring, attitude, fuel and the paint you flew with); your Daily Drop results for the current and previous day (attempts, best score, best flight card); whether you chose to use Game Center and any score still waiting to be posted (section 3c); and when the app last asked you for a rating (section 3e). This information is stored in the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

2. What the app does not do

Padfall does not require an account and has no analytics of its own. It does not ask to track you across other apps and websites, does not access your location services, camera, microphone, photos or contacts, and does not send your gameplay or settings over the internet. Version 1.0 shows no ads at all; from version 1.1, Padfall shows ads (section 3). From version 1.3 the only gameplay information that can leave your device is a leaderboard score you post through Game Center (section 3c) or a flight card you choose to share (section 3d); Padfall has no server of its own and receives neither.

If a future version adds any other service that collects information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

3. Advertising (version 1.1 and later)

Starting with version 1.1, Padfall is supported by advertising: a small banner on the main menu and an occasional fullscreen ad after a landing or crash (never during a flight), served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource mediation and Unity Ads, operated by Unity Technologies). No purchase removes ads; ads stop only if you withdraw consent to the advertising service under Privacy options (see below).

Before the first ad, Padfall asks once whether ads may be personalized; the question also tells you that running the advertising service stores and reads information on your device and sends ad requests with device information, and that either answer starts it. If you decide later, the advertising service is not started and no ads are requested until you answer, so nothing new is stored or sent for ads. Personalization is never switched on without your answer. If you choose Non-personalized ads only, the advertising service is told that you have not consented and serves non-personalized ads; if you choose Allow personalized ads, it may use your device's advertising identifiers to personalize ads. You can also choose Not now: then no ads are requested until you answer, the game plays normally, and the question returns at the next launch. You can change or withdraw your answer at any time under Privacy options on the main menu: the advertising service is told immediately and the new answer applies to every ad requested from then on; ads already loaded under the previous answer are discarded without being shown, and ads continue in the mode you chose. If the advertising service cannot be told of your change, no ads are requested until it has been.

Running the advertising service at all means storing and reading information on your device and sending ad requests, which happens only after you answer the question. You can withdraw that consent under Privacy options ("Withdraw consent to the ad service"): Padfall then stops requesting ads at once, discards loaded ads, and does not start the advertising service at later launches; the game stays fully playable, and you can consent again from the same place. One limitation: the advertising software already running in the current session cannot be shut down by the app, so information it has already sent or holds in memory is not affected until you next launch Padfall. If you chose "No ads" in an earlier 1.1 test build, Padfall asks the question again and requests no ads until you answer; that earlier choice is never read as permission to process data or to personalize.

To serve and measure ads, Unity's software in the app processes information such as your device's advertising and vendor identifiers (Padfall does not request tracking permission, so the advertising identifier is not available to it), device and app information, coarse location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with ads (views, clicks). Unity uses this information under its own privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy (game-player section: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy), which also explains your choices, including Unity's data access and opt-out options. We do not receive personal information from the advertising service; we receive aggregated reporting. Padfall signals "do not sell or share" to the advertising service for everyone and does not flag the app as directed at children (it is not).

3a. In-app purchases (paint schemes, from version 1.1)

Padfall sells optional paint schemes for the booster as one-time (non-consumable) in-app purchases through Apple. Apple handles the payment, your Apple Account and any authentication; Padfall never sees your payment details. The app receives from Apple only the verified purchase record for each paint (the product identifier and its status) and stores on your device which paints you own and which one you equipped. Padfall sends none of this anywhere, reports no purchase to the advertising service, and uses no analytics. "Restore purchases" in the Paint shop asks Apple for your purchases on the same Apple Account. A paint purchase changes nothing about advertising: it grants no advertising consent, does not remove ads and does not affect your privacy choices. Refunds are handled by Apple; a refunded paint disappears from the shop and the booster returns to Original Silver.

From version 1.3 the shop also offers the Country Paint Collection, one non-consumable purchase that grants the thirteen country paints; it is handled exactly like a single paint (Apple's purchase record, stored on your device, nothing sent anywhere), and a refunded collection removes only the paints you do not own individually. The Nightfall paint is free for everyone and involves no purchase.

3b. Daily Drop and flight cards (version 1.3 and later): stored on your device

The Daily Drop is one landing challenge per day, the same for everyone; the day changes at 00:00 UTC. The challenge is computed inside the app from the date — nothing is downloaded for it. Your attempts, your best score of the day and the flight card of that best landing are stored on your device only (section 1), as are the flight cards of your best campaign landings. A flight card contains only that flight's own numbers (mission, grade, score, softness, centring, attitude, fuel left, impact and offset) and the name of the paint you flew with; it contains no account, device or personal information.

3c. Game Center leaderboards (version 1.3 and later): optional, run by Apple

Padfall offers two leaderboards through Apple's Game Center: today's best Daily Drop score and your all-time banked Cadence score. Game Center is optional. Padfall does not contact Game Center until you open Leaderboards for the first time; playing, the Daily Drop and your local bests never require it. If your device is not signed in to Game Center, Padfall shows a Sign in button and presents Apple's own sign-in only when you tap it.

Once you have opened Leaderboards, Padfall posts your Daily Drop best for the current day and your banked Cadence score to Game Center, and reads the leaderboards back to show them. What Game Center receives is the score, the game, the day it belongs to and your Game Center player identity; what Padfall shows is what Game Center returns — ranks, the public display names of other players as Apple provides them, and scores. Apple runs Game Center under Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy; Game Center details: apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center). We do not run a server, do not receive your scores or identity from Apple, and cannot see who is on the leaderboards beyond what the app shows you. Padfall keeps on your device only your choice to use Game Center and a score that could not yet be posted (for example offline); a Daily Drop score is never posted after its day has ended.

To stop using Game Center, sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings; Padfall then posts nothing and shows the Sign in button again. Scores already posted are managed by Apple through your Apple Account and Game Center settings.

3d. Sharing a flight card (version 1.3 and later): your choice of destination

A flight card can be turned into an image and shared with the Share flight card button, which opens the standard iOS share sheet. The image contains only the card's own flight numbers and the game's name, with no account, device or personal information. Where it goes is entirely your choice (Messages, Mail, Photos, another app); Padfall writes the image into its own temporary storage on your device for the share sheet and sends it nowhere itself. We do not receive shared cards.

3e. Rating prompt (version 1.3 and later)

Occasionally, after a perfect landing, Padfall may ask iOS to show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt (at most once per app version and not more than once in sixty days, never during an ad or a purchase). The prompt and anything you enter in it are handled by Apple and the App Store; Padfall only remembers on your device when it last asked. Rating is never required and never rewarded.

4. Email you send us, and this website

The app's Privacy and Support links open this website in your browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages themselves set no cookies and contain no analytics or third-party content. GitHub, the host, logs and stores the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub (GitHub's documentation: "When a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor has signed into GitHub or not" — docs.github.com). That logging is done by GitHub under GitHub's Privacy Statement, not by us; we do not receive those logs.

If you email us at skyflip2026@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to handle your request and reply to you. We keep correspondence about this game for at most 12 months after your request is resolved, and we delete it earlier when it is no longer needed. Two narrow exceptions: if an issue you raised is still open, we keep that thread until it is resolved, and the 12-month limit then runs from that point; and if a law requires us to keep a particular message for longer, we keep that message only for as long as the law requires.

You can ask us to delete your correspondence with us about this game at any time by emailing the same address — please put "Deletion request" in the subject line so we can find it. We handle deletion requests separately from routine support, and where a law sets a deadline for answering such a request, that deadline applies.

Deleting your data — by kind

  1. Deleting Padfall from your device removes its saved progress and settings from that device; "offloading" the app (an iOS feature) removes the app but keeps its documents and data, so progress returns if you reinstall. The same applies to the record of which paint schemes you own and which one you equipped: deleting the app removes that record from the device, while the purchases themselves stay with Apple and come back through "Restore purchases" in the Paint shop on the same Apple Account (the equipped choice does not, and refunded purchases do not return). From version 1.3, deleting the app also removes the flight cards, the Daily Drop results, the Game Center choice and the rating-prompt record kept on the device, and the temporary image of a shared flight card. Backup behaviour is controlled by iOS; we make no promises about device backups.
  2. Scores you posted to Game Center leaderboards (version 1.3 and later) are held by Apple, not by us; manage them through your Apple Account and Game Center settings under Apple's privacy policy (section 3c).
  3. Emails you exchanged with us about this game are deleted on request at skyflip2026@gmail.com, and otherwise at most 12 months after your request is resolved — earlier when no longer needed, subject to the two narrow exceptions in section 4.

Children

Padfall is not offered in the App Store's Kids category and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal information through the app ourselves, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. From version 1.1, the advertising service in the app processes the information described in section 3 to serve and measure ads, and optional paint purchases are handled by Apple (section 3a). From version 1.3, Game Center leaderboards are optional and handled by Apple under Apple's own rules for Game Center and child accounts (section 3c). Padfall has no accounts or chat of its own; ads (version 1.1 and later) are not directed at children.
