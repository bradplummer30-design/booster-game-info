Support — Padfall

Padfall is a portrait booster-landing game for iPhone and iPad. It requires iOS 15 or later. Native iPad support requires version 1.1 or later. It plays fully offline and has no accounts of its own. Version 1.1 is supported by ads and sells optional paint schemes for the booster as one-time in-app purchases; version 1.0 has no ads and no purchases. Version 1.3 adds the free Nightfall paint, the Country Paint Collection, the Daily Drop, optional Game Center leaderboards and shareable flight cards.

Controls

  • Left pad (Attitude): drag left or right to tilt the nose. Left tilts the nose left, and the engine pushes you that way.
  • Right pad (Thrust): drag up for more thrust. Lifting your finger cuts the engine — it does not latch.
  • Pause: the button at the top right. Resume, Retry or Quit.

Landing tips

  • The bracket on the altitude tape marks the last altitude where a full vertical burn can still stop you at the pad. Start the burn there, then ease off as the descent slows.
  • The diamond on the ground shows where you would hit if you never thrust. Kill sideways drift before the burn.
  • Touchdown must be soft, upright and on the deck. The limits for each mission are shown under the instruments.

Progress

  • Missions unlock one at a time as you land them. Cadence is a separate chain of drops with a growing multiplier.
  • Progress and settings are saved on this device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Paint shop and purchases (version 1.1 and later)

"Shop" on the main menu opens the Paint shop, which shows the booster in each paint. Original Silver is always free. The thirteen country-inspired paints are one-time purchases through Apple at the price shown for your region. Tapping a paint only previews it; "Buy" opens Apple's purchase sheet, and after the purchase the paint shows "Owned" with an "Equip" button. Equip it, or return to Original Silver, in the same screen; the choice is kept across launches. Paints are looks only and give no gameplay advantage.

If a paint you bought is missing (a new device, a reinstall, or a purchase that completed later, such as Ask to Buy), tap "Restore purchases" in the Paint shop while signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. Refunds are requested from Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com); a refunded paint is removed and the booster returns to Original Silver. Purchases never change the advertising settings.

Version 1.3. Nightfall, the Halloween paint, is free for everyone: it shows "Free" in the shop and "Equip" puts it on the booster; nothing is auto-equipped, and it stays available after Halloween. The Country Paint Collection is one purchase (its localized price is shown in its "Buy" button) that grants all thirteen country paints; after a completed purchase every included paint shows "Equip" and the collection shows "Owned". The shop tells you how many of the thirteen you already own and, when buying the remaining ones individually would cost less in your storefront, says so; paints you already bought are not refunded by the collection. Prices appear only in "Buy" buttons; "Restore purchases" brings the collection back on a new device.

Daily Drop, leaderboards and flight cards (version 1.3)

  • Daily Drop on the main menu is one landing challenge per day, the same for everyone, with unlimited retries; your best score of the day counts. The day changes at 00:00 UTC (the sheet shows the countdown). It needs no account and no connection; your best is kept on this device.
  • Leaderboards (from the Daily Drop sheet) are Apple's Game Center boards: DAILY for today's best Daily Drop scores and CADENCE for all-time banked Cadence scores. They are optional: Padfall does not use Game Center until you open Leaderboards, and shows a "Sign in to Game Center" button if your device is not signed in. A Daily Drop score is posted only on the day it was flown; if you were offline, it is posted the next time you are signed in on that day. Game Center sign-in, display names and friends are managed in iOS Settings → Game Center.
  • Flight cards: after a landing, "Flight card" on the result sheet shows a card with the mission, grade, score, softness, centring, attitude and fuel left; your best landing per campaign mission keeps its card (the "Card" button on the Campaign sheet; older bests from before 1.3 have none). "Share flight card" opens the iOS share sheet with the card image; the image carries only the flight's numbers.
  • Rating prompt: occasionally after a perfect landing Padfall may show Apple's rating prompt; it is never required.

Ads (version 1.1)

Padfall is supported by ads: a banner sits at the top of the main menu and a fullscreen ad can appear after a landing or crash, never during a flight. There is no setting that turns ads off and no purchase that removes them. Before the first ad, Padfall asks once whether ads may be personalized (Allow personalized ads or Non-personalized ads only); nothing is served until you answer, and personalization is never switched on without your answer. Not now closes the question for this launch: no ads are requested, the game plays, and the question returns next time. Privacy options on the main menu shows your current answer and lets you change or withdraw it at any time — the advertising service is told immediately and ads continue in the mode you chose. It also lets you withdraw your consent to the advertising service as a whole: Padfall then stops requesting ads, discards loaded ads and does not start the service at later launches (software already running in the current session stops only at the next launch); you can consent again there. Padfall does not request permission to track you across other apps and websites. Details: privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound: check the SOUND setting on the title screen, the ring/silent switch and the volume. Sound starts after your first tap.
  • Controls feel stuck after switching apps: controls are cleared when the app loses focus; touch the pads again.
