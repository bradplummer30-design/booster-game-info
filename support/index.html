Support — Padfall

Padfall is a portrait booster-landing game for iPhone running iOS 15 or later. It plays fully offline and has no accounts or purchases. Version 1.1 is supported by ads; version 1.0 has no ads.

Controls

  • Left pad (Attitude): drag left or right to tilt the nose. Left tilts the nose left, and the engine pushes you that way.
  • Right pad (Thrust): drag up for more thrust. Lifting your finger cuts the engine — it does not latch.
  • Pause: the button at the top right. Resume, Retry or Quit.

Landing tips

  • The bracket on the altitude tape marks the last altitude where a full vertical burn can still stop you at the pad. Start the burn there, then ease off as the descent slows.
  • The diamond on the ground shows where you would hit if you never thrust. Kill sideways drift before the burn.
  • Touchdown must be soft, upright and on the deck. The limits for each mission are shown under the instruments.

Progress

  • Missions unlock one at a time as you land them. Cadence is a separate chain of drops with a growing multiplier.
  • Progress and settings are saved on this device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (version 1.1)

Padfall is supported by ads: a banner sits at the top of the main menu and a fullscreen ad can appear after a landing or crash, never during a flight. There is no setting that turns ads off and no purchase that removes them. Before the first ad, Padfall asks once whether ads may be personalized (Allow personalized ads or Non-personalized ads only); nothing is served until you answer, and personalization is never switched on without your answer. Not now closes the question for this launch: no ads are requested, the game plays, and the question returns next time. Privacy options on the main menu shows your current answer and lets you change or withdraw it at any time — the advertising service is told immediately and ads continue in the mode you chose. It also lets you withdraw your consent to the advertising service as a whole: Padfall then stops requesting ads, discards loaded ads and does not start the service at later launches (software already running in the current session stops only at the next launch); you can consent again there. Padfall does not request permission to track you across other apps and websites. Details: privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound: check the SOUND setting on the title screen, the ring/silent switch and the volume. Sound starts after your first tap.
  • Controls feel stuck after switching apps: controls are cleared when the app loses focus; touch the pads again.
